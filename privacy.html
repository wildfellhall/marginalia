Privacy policy

Marginalia is published by Sanah Rajesh.

Effective September 12, 2026 · Marginalia version 1.1.1

Marginalia stores your reading library and annotations locally in your browser profile. It has no account system, analytics, advertising, cloud sync, or developer-operated data server.

Information the extension handles

  • Annotations you save: selected passage text, up to 40 characters of surrounding text on each side to locate the passage again, notes, labels, colors, annotation type, page or scroll position, and save/edit timestamps.
  • Reading sources: titles, authors or website hostnames, saved page URLs (including query parameters), file names, formats, and dates a saved document is opened.
  • Files you import: original PDF files and sanitized text and images extracted from DRM-free EPUBs, HTML, text, or Markdown files. Marginalia reads only files you select or drop into its import area.
  • Organization: folder names, colors, and the readings or annotations assigned to them.

Passages, notes, and files may contain personal or sensitive information if you choose to save such content. Marginalia does not request account credentials, payment details, your browser history database, cookies, location, contacts, or access to unrelated files.

Why website access is needed

On HTTP and HTTPS pages where Chrome allows the extension to run, Marginalia checks the current URL against saved reading records. On pages with saved annotations, it reads text to restore the selected passages. When you enable the toolbar, it reads the text you select to create annotations. The extension monitors changes to an open page so existing highlights can follow content changes.

A page URL, title, or passage is saved only when you choose to save a website or annotation. Marginalia does not keep a general record of your browsing history or monitor keystrokes in page forms. Browser-internal pages and some protected or embedded content are inaccessible.

Storage, use, and sharing

Saved annotations, folder names, colors, memberships, and document metadata use Chrome's local extension storage. PDF files use the extension's IndexedDB storage. The separate web development preview uses localStorage and IndexedDB under its own origin. This data is used to display, search, edit, export, and restore your reading and annotations.

The extension does not automatically upload your library to the publisher or operate a developer data server. Marginalia does not sell data, create advertising profiles, or train AI models with your data. This does not make the on-page panel private from the website, as explained below. Fonts, libraries, and PDF rendering resources are packaged with the extension; it does not fetch executable code from a CDN.

When you open a saved source or external help link, the destination website receives the normal browser request and applies its own privacy practices. If you contact support, the contact service you choose processes the information you send; do not send private notes or full backups unless you want to share them.

Visibility of on-page notes

The on-page annotation panel is not isolated from the website. Website scripts can read notes, labels, and other content placed in that panel, including content that remains in the page when the panel is hidden. A website may collect or transmit information it can access according to its own practices. Do not enter passwords, financial details, or other confidential information into the on-page panel.

Backups and device security

Export creates an unencrypted JSON file in your downloads location containing your reading records, annotations, labels, folders, imported content, and PDF files. Import reads the backup you select and merges it with local records. Marginalia does not upload backups or automatically sync them. You control whether to move or share them.

Local storage and backups are not password protected or encrypted by Marginalia. People or software with access to your device or browser profile may be able to access them. On-page highlights and notes are displayed in the page's document; do not use them to store secrets on websites you do not trust. Use your device's security controls and keep backups in a private location.

Retention, access, and deletion

Data stays in your browser profile until you delete it, clear the extension's data, or uninstall the extension. You can view and edit records in the library, delete individual annotations, export your library, or choose Settings & backup → Delete all local reading data and confirm to erase the local library and imported PDF files. This action does not delete backup files you already downloaded. Delete those separately in your file manager if desired.

Removing the extension or clearing browser storage can remove local data. Keep a backup if you need to retain it. A browser or operating-system backup of your profile is controlled by that browser or operating system.

Chrome Web Store Limited Use

Marginalia limits its use of user data to its disclosed reading and annotation features, consistent with the Chrome Web Store Limited Use requirements. It does not sell or transfer user data for advertising, creditworthiness, lending, or unrelated purposes. The on-page visibility and user-initiated sharing described in this policy still apply.

Changes and contact

Changes to data practices will be reflected in an updated policy and, where needed, an in-product notice before the changed practices take effect. For support or privacy questions, contact Sanah Rajesh at leapon.outreach@gmail.com.
