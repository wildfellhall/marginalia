MARGINALIA FOR CHROME & EDGE

Your readings
and notes.

Annotate course readings and find your notes when you need them.

Marginalia is a Chrome and Edge extension for annotating websites and reading imported PDFs and DRM-free EPUBs. Highlight key passages, write questions or explanations, and label notes for assignments or exam review.

Saved on your device. No account, advertising, analytics, or automatic cloud sync. Export your library when you want a backup or a move to another device.

Reading and study tools

  • Highlights in six pastel shades or a custom color.
  • Underlines, margin notes, sticky notes, flags, tabs, and bookmarks.
  • A library searchable by highlighted text, notes, and labels.
  • Folders for courses, assignments, or topics, with readings and individual notes.
  • PDF and EPUB readers alongside an on-page website annotation panel.
  • Export and import for your annotations and reading files.

On permitted websites, Marginalia checks page URLs and reads text to restore saved highlights. It saves the source URL, selected passage, surrounding text, and notes when you choose to save an annotation. Read the privacy policy for details.

Requires Chrome or Chromium-based Edge version 140 or later. PDFs must be imported into the reader. DRM removal and OCR are not included.
