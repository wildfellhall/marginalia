# Chrome Web Store listing — Marginalia 1.1.1

## Name

Marginalia — Study Notes & Highlights

## Summary (manifest description)

Highlight websites, PDFs, and EPUBs. Take reading notes, organize by course, and search your annotations.

## Detailed description

Marginalia lets you highlight course readings, add notes, and find them again when you are writing a paper or studying for an exam. Use it on articles, websites, and Project Gutenberg HTML books, or import PDFs and DRM-free EPUBs into your library.

ANNOTATE YOUR READINGS
• Highlight in six pastel colors or choose your own.
• Underline passages and add margin notes, sticky notes, flags, tabs, and bookmarks.
• Add explanations, discussion questions, or reminders alongside the text.
• Label notes with topics such as “Key terms,” “Essay evidence,” or “Review.” Search labels, highlighted text, and your own notes.
• Return to a website and see your saved highlights again.

ORGANIZE BY COURSE OR ASSIGNMENT
• Create folders for classes, papers, or topics. A reading or annotation can belong to several folders.
• Search within a folder, rename it, or remove it without deleting your reading data. Folders are included in backups.
• Read imported PDFs with selectable text and page navigation.
• Read DRM-free EPUBs with chapters and embedded book images.
• Import HTML, text, and Markdown files; Markdown appears as plain text.
• Adding a reading to a folder includes its annotations, including notes you add later.

SAVED ON YOUR DEVICE
Your library is stored in your browser profile. No account, advertising, analytics, or automatic cloud sync. Export a backup of your readings, annotations, labels, folders, and original PDF files. Import it to restore your work or transfer it to another device.

WHY WEBSITE ACCESS?
Marginalia checks the current page URL and reads text on permitted websites to restore annotations and capture passages you select. It saves a page URL, title, passage, surrounding text, and notes when you choose to save them. It does not keep a general browsing-history log or send your library to the developer.

GOOD TO KNOW
PDFs must be imported into Marginalia's reader rather than annotated in Chrome's built-in PDF viewer. Scanned PDFs need an existing text layer for highlights; page notes and bookmarks still work. EPUBs must be DRM-free. Browser settings, extension stores, closed shadow roots, and embedded cross-origin frames cannot be annotated directly. Changes to a website may prevent a saved passage from being located again, but its quote stays in your library.

Requires Chrome 140 or later. Import limit: 75 MB per file; EPUB extracted content is limited to 20 MB. Export backups before uninstalling or clearing browser data. Backups are unencrypted, so keep them somewhere private.
